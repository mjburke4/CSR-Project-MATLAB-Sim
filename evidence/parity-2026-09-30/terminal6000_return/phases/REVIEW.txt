FULL 6,000-SECOND TERMINAL CANDIDATE: INDEPENDENT CUSTODY-PHASE REVIEW
2026-09-30

Verdict
The remaining source-standardized mean latency difference is almost entirely
NWK waiting: +26.251732649 s total = +26.197863248 s NWK waiting,
-0.252737119 s initial MAC waiting, and +0.306606520 s after first physical TX.
Combined MAC/HOP service differs by only +0.053869401 s on this standardized
population. This decomposition is descriptive, not proof that MAC/feedback
behavior cannot cause upstream NWK waiting.

Evidence and method
reconstruct.py independently reads both original native compressed traces and
both new MATLAB protocol traces. All 25,090 native applications agree with the
issued reference ledger on source, generation, and delivered/unresolved status.
Every one of 24,055 native first deliveries and delivered-app NWK totals agrees
exactly at nanosecond resolution with that ledger. All 23,720 MATLAB deliveries
agree with the returned application ledger. Every delivered hop has a HOP
admission and first physical DATA transmission; all 47,775 delivered application
paths add exactly to first-unique-delivery latency after timestamp rounding to
integer nanoseconds.

An application ID is used only within its own engine/run. Autonomous engines
are not paired by numeric seed or local ID. Means are calculated for each
engine's actual delivered population, and then standardized with native
source-by-seed delivery weights. The three native-zero source cells are not
included in defined relative-latency comparisons.

Each accepted custody hop starts at NWK enqueue. HOP admission ends NWK waiting;
first physical DATA TX ends initial MAC waiting; arrival at the next accepted
custody or final first delivery ends the remainder. The latter includes actual
transmission, retry/feedback effects and queueing after first TX; it is not an
isolated retry-timer metric. Owner expiry never ends or restarts the application
clock. Native repeated copies are disambiguated by receiving HOP feedback
sequence and actual first-delivery ancestry, with FIFO assignment of NWK copies
to their HOP admissions. Seed132 has seven duplicate NWK enqueue events. Summing
all branches would overcount latency. Every native DATA TX mapped to an installed
HOP sequence; that map persists after HOP owner expiration. MATLAB hop_sent is
emitted only while an owner exists, so later DATA sent counts are lower bounds;
first-TX coverage is complete for every delivered hop.

Unfinished population
Native has 1,035 unresolved applications: 284 last recorded in NWK waiting,
43 awaiting first physical TX, and 708 after first physical TX.
MATLAB has 1,278 unresolved applications: 451, 48, and 779 in those stages.
These are last-recorded custody stages, not proof of all-copy liveness or final
loss. For a native application with multiple unresolved copies, the displayed
path follows the latest recorded accepted custody. Provisional terminal/drop
callbacks do not reset age and do not prove no queued/on-air copy can finish.
